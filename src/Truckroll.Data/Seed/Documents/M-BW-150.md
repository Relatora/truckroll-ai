# Breezeway BW-150 Heat Recovery Ventilator

A heat recovery ventilator (HRV) that exchanges stale indoor air for fresh outdoor air while
recovering about 70% of the heat. Rated 150 CFM. Required in most new Ontario homes.

## Troubleshooting

### Rattling or vibration

Lint and debris build up on the fan wheels and throw them out of balance. Clean the core and both
fan wheels. Check the unit's hanging straps haven't loosened.

### Condensation or ice in winter

The defrost cycle isn't running or the condensate drain is blocked. Check the drain loop is
primed with water and slopes down. Confirm the defrost damper moves.

### Stuffy house, high humidity

The unit may be set too low or the filters are clogged, cutting airflow. Clean the filters and
check the wall control setting.

## Maintenance

Every three months: wash the filters. Every fall: remove and wash the heat recovery core in warm
soapy water, clean the outdoor hoods of leaves and snow, and pour water into the drain pan to
confirm it drains.
