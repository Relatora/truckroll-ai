# Aquavane Limited Warranty

Covers Aquavane AV-50 tank and AVT-199 tankless water heaters. This summary is for staff; the
certificate that came with the unit governs.

## Coverage

| Component                      | AV-50 tank | AVT-199 tankless |
| ------------------------------ | ---------- | ---------------- |
| Tank / heat exchanger          | 6 years    | 12 years         |
| Parts                          | 6 years    | 5 years          |
| Labour                         | 1 year     | 1 year           |

## Scale damage is not covered

Aquavane does not cover heat exchanger damage caused by scale. On a tankless unit in a hard-water
area, the warranty requires either a water softener or a documented yearly descale. Record every
descale on the job so the customer has proof if they ever need to claim.

## Making a claim

Provide the serial number, proof of the install date, and for AVT-199 heat exchanger claims, the
descaling history.
