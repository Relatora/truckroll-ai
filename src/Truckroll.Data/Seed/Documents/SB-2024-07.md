# SB-2024-07: CAL-80 Hot Surface Ignitor Cracking

## Affected units

Calderon CAL-80-60, CAL-80-80 and CAL-80-100 furnaces with serial numbers beginning CAL80-19,
CAL80-20 or CAL80-21 (built 2019 to 2021).

## The problem

The ignitor bracket on these units holds the element about 3 mm too close to the burner, so the
ignitor runs hotter than designed and develops hairline cracks at the base. Affected units can
crack an ignitor every one to two heating seasons. Symptoms: the blower runs but the burners never
light, and the board shows one flash (ignition lockout).

## The fix

Replace the ignitor with IGN-80H **and** fit the revised bracket kit BK-80R, which moves the
element back to the correct position. Replacing the ignitor alone does not stop it happening
again.

## Coverage

For affected serial numbers, Calderon pays for the ignitor, the bracket kit and one hour of
labour, regardless of the unit's normal warranty status, until December 31, 2027. File the claim
under SB-2024-07, not under the standard warranty.
