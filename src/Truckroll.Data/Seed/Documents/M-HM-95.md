# Hearthmark HM-95 Gas Furnace

A 95% AFUE single-stage condensing furnace with a belt-driven blower. Sold until 2014 and
still common in older homes. Sizes HM-95-60 and HM-95-80. Gas work requires a G2 licence.

## Specifications

| Model    | Input (BTU/h) | Output (BTU/h) | Blower          | Fuse on board |
| -------- | ------------- | -------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| HM-95-60 | 60,000        | 57,000         | Belt drive, 1/3 hp | 3 A        |
| HM-95-80 | 80,000        | 76,000         | Belt drive, 1/2 hp | 3 A        |

## Troubleshooting

### No heat and no status light

Check the 3 A automotive fuse on the control board. A blown fuse usually means a shorted
thermostat wire: look for damage where the wire runs through joists, often from rodents. Repair
the wire before replacing the fuse, or the new fuse will blow too.

### Squealing or slapping noise from the blower

The blower belt is worn or loose. Replace belt HB-A31 and set tension to about 1/2 inch of
deflection. Oil both blower bearings (three drops of SAE 20 non-detergent oil).

### Short cycling

Clean the flame sensor and check the condensate trap, as on any condensing furnace.

## End of life

Parts for the HM-95 are getting scarce, and the heat exchanger on units over 15 years old should
be inspected carefully for cracks. When repair costs approach a third of a new furnace, present a
replacement estimate.
