# Calderon and Hearthmark Limited Warranty

Calderon and Hearthmark are sister brands and share one warranty. This summary is for staff;
the certificate that came with the unit governs.

## Coverage

| Component                        | Calderon CAL-80, CAL-96 | Hearthmark HM-95, HMB-110 |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Heat exchanger                   | Lifetime (original owner) | 20 years                |
| Functional parts                 | 10 years                | 10 years                  |
| Labour                           | Not covered             | Not covered               |

Functional parts cover the ignitor, flame sensor, gas valve, control board, inducer and pressure
switches. Filters, belts and fuses are wear items and are not covered.

## Registration

The 10-year parts term requires online registration within 60 days of installation. Unregistered
units get 5 years. Kestrel registers every unit we install (see policy P-104).

## Making a claim

The installing contractor files the claim with the unit's serial number, the failed part's
number and the date of failure. Parts are shipped to the branch within 5 business days; we use
our truck stock in the meantime and swap the warranty part back into stock when it arrives.

## Exclusions

Damage from flooding, improper venting, unqualified repairs, or running without a filter.
