# Calderon CAL-96 Series Condensing Gas Furnace

High-efficiency (96% AFUE), two-stage condensing gas furnace with a variable-speed blower.
Sizes CAL-96-60, CAL-96-80 and CAL-96-100. Venting is Category IV PVC, direct vent or single
pipe. Gas work requires a G2 licence or higher.

## Condensate system

A condensing furnace produces up to 2 gallons of acidic water a day in cold weather. It drains
through a trap on the inducer housing to a floor drain or a condensate pump. **Most service calls
on this series are condensate problems**, not burner problems.

- The trap must be primed (full of water) or flue gas pushes through it and the pressure switch
  opens.
- The drain line must slope down all the way. An uphill run lets water back up into the trap.
- In a cold snap, lines that run through an unheated space or out through a wall can freeze.

| 1       | Ignition lockout                             | Ignitor, flame sensor, gas supply            |
| 3       | Low-fire pressure switch did not close       | **Condensate trap**, vent blockage, inducer  |
| 4       | High limit open                              | Filter, blower, closed registers             |
| 7       | High-fire pressure switch did not close      | Condensate trap, vent length, inducer        |
| 8       | Blower motor communication fault             | Motor plug, board                            |

## Troubleshooting

### Furnace shuts off with a gurgling sound

Classic blocked or unprimed condensate trap. Remove the trap, flush it, re-prime it with water
and check the drain line slope. If the trap keeps filling with sludge, flush the secondary heat
exchanger drain as well.

### Water under the furnace

Check the condensate pump: hose on, float free, discharge line clear. Then check the trap
gaskets and the vent pipe for a leaking joint.

### Blowing cold air

Look at the status code first. Code 3 or 7 points to the condensate system or the inducer. A
noisy or slow inducer should be replaced as an assembly (IND-96).

## Maintenance

Each fall: replace the 4-inch media filter, remove and flush the condensate trap, check the
drain line and pump, inspect the vent and intake terminations outside (snow, wasp nests), clean
the flame sensor and verify combustion.
