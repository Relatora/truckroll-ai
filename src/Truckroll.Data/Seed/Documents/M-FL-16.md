# Frostline FL-16 Central Air Conditioner

A 16 SEER2 single-stage split air conditioner. Units built from 2025 use R-454B refrigerant;
earlier units use R-410A. Sizes FL-16-24, FL-16-30 and FL-16-36 (cooling capacity in thousands
of BTU/h, so 2, 2.5 and 3 tons). Refrigerant work requires a 313A licence.

## Troubleshooting

### Outdoor unit hums but the fan doesn't spin

The dual run capacitor has failed, which is by far the most common summer call on this unit.
Heat waves accelerate it. With power off and the capacitor discharged, measure both sides: a
reading more than 6% below the rating means replace. A bulged top is a giveaway.

### Running but not cooling the house

Check the filter and indoor coil first. Then measure subcooling: the target is 10 °F ± 2. Low
subcooling means a low charge from a leak. The most common leak points are the service valve
cores and the indoor coil's U-bends. Find and fix the leak before recharging; never top up.

### Ice on the suction line

Low airflow is the usual cause: blocked filter, closed registers, dirty indoor coil. Turn the
system to fan only to thaw the coil before testing the charge.

## Maintenance

Each spring: clean the outdoor coil from the inside out, check the capacitor, measure amp draw on
the compressor and fan, check the condensate drain on the indoor coil, and record subcooling and
superheat.
