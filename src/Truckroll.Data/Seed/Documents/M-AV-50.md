# Aquavane AV-50 Gas Tank Water Heater

A 50 US gallon (189 L) atmospheric-vent gas water heater with a standing pilot. Input 40,000
BTU/h. Gas work requires a G2 licence or higher.

## Troubleshooting

### Pilot won't stay lit

The thermocouple is the usual culprit. Measure its output with the pilot lit: below about
20 millivolts it should be replaced. Also check the pilot flame engulfs the top 10 to 13 mm of
the thermocouple and that the pilot orifice isn't partly blocked.

### Not enough hot water

Sediment on the tank bottom insulates the water from the burner. Flush the tank. Check the dip
tube isn't broken (plastic bits in the aerators are a sign).

### Leaking

Water from the relief valve discharge pipe means the valve is opening: check the pressure and
the thermostat setting, and whether a thermal expansion tank is needed. Water from the bottom of
the tank itself means the tank has failed. It can't be repaired; quote a replacement.

## Life expectancy

Typical tank life is 8 to 12 years. The anode rod protects the tank; checking it every three
years and replacing it when it's down to the core wire extends tank life.

## Maintenance

Yearly: flush sediment, test the relief valve, check the vent draft with a match or smoke at the
draft hood, inspect the anode rod every third year.
