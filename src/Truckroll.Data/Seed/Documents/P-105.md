# P-105 Refrigerant Handling and Records

## Who can do it

Only technicians holding a 313A licence and an Ozone Depletion Prevention card may connect gauges
to a refrigerant circuit, recover, or charge. Everyone else may change filters and clean coils.

## Never top up a leak

Find and repair the leak before charging. "Topping up" a leaking system is against the
regulations and just brings the customer back next month.

## Records

For every job that adds or recovers refrigerant, note on the job:

- the refrigerant type (R-410A or R-454B),
- the amount added or recovered,
- where the leak was and how it was repaired.

Recovered refrigerant goes back to the branch in a labelled recovery cylinder.

## R-454B

Units built from 2025 use R-454B, which is mildly flammable (A2L). Use A2L-rated recovery
equipment and leak detectors, and never braze on a charged system.
