# Aquavane AVT-199 Tankless Water Heater

A condensing on-demand gas water heater, 199,000 BTU/h input, up to 9.8 US gal/min. Vents in
PVC. Gas work requires a G2 licence or higher.

## Error codes

| Code | Meaning                                         | First check                                       |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| E-03 | Ignition failure                                | Gas supply, igniter, flame rod                    |
| E-10 | Exhaust or intake blocked                       | Vent terminations, condensate drain               |
| E-12 | Heat exchanger overheat / scale detected        | **Scale on the heat exchanger**; descale          |
| E-16 | Water flow sensor fault                         | Inlet filter, flow sensor turbine (see SB-2025-03) |
| E-25 | Condensate trap full                            | Condensate drain                                  |

## Hard water

Above about 7 grains per gallon (120 mg/L) of hardness, scale builds on the heat exchanger and
the unit runs hot and throws E-12. Guelph's municipal water is hard (typically 25 to 30 grains),
so units there need descaling every year without a softener, and every two to three years with
one.

## Descaling procedure

1. Close the hot and cold isolation valves and turn off the gas.
2. Connect a circulation pump and hoses to the service ports.
3. Circulate 4 litres of food-grade white vinegar or the Aquavane descaler for 45 minutes.
4. Flush with clean water for 5 minutes, clean the inlet filter screen, and restore the valves.
5. Clear the code and run hot water at full flow to confirm stable outlet temperature.

## Troubleshooting

### Hot water goes cold mid-shower

Usually scale (E-12) or a fouled flow sensor (E-16). On units built before 2025, the original
flow sensor turbine collects scale easily; SB-2025-03 describes the updated sensor.

### Lukewarm water at all taps

Check for a cross-connection (a mixing valve or a single-handle faucet letting cold into the hot
line), then check the flow sensor.
