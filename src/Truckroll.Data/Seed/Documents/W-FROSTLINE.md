# Frostline Limited Warranty

Covers Frostline FL-16 air conditioners and FHP-20 heat pumps. This summary is for staff; the
certificate that came with the unit governs.

## Coverage

| Component          | Term      |
| ------------------ | --------- |
| Compressor         | 10 years  |
| Functional parts   | 10 years  |
| Refrigerant        | Not covered |
| Labour             | Not covered |

Capacitors and contactors are functional parts and are covered.

## Registration

Must be registered within 90 days of installation, or coverage drops to 5 years.

## Making a claim

Provide the outdoor unit's serial number, the date of failure, and for compressor claims, the
recorded superheat and subcooling readings and amp draw. A compressor claim without readings will
be denied.
