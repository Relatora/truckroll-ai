# P-102 Comfort Club Membership Benefits

## Plans

| Benefit                              | Comfort Club ($19.99/mo) | Comfort Club Plus ($29.99/mo) |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Fall heating tune-up                 | Yes                      | Yes                           |
| Spring cooling tune-up               | Yes                      | Yes                           |
| Priority booking                     | Yes                      | Yes                           |
| Repair discount                      | 10%                      | 15%                           |
| Diagnostic fee ($99)                 | Charged                  | Waived                        |
| After-hours fee                      | Half                     | Waived                        |
| Labour on manufacturer-warranty repairs | Covered               | Covered                       |

## Renewal

Plans renew yearly on their start date and bill monthly. A plan lapses after two missed
payments. The CSR calls lapsed members once before the plan is cancelled.

## Tune-up scheduling

The fall tune-up runs from mid-September to the end of October, and the spring tune-up from
mid-April to the end of May. Members who haven't booked by the first of the second month get a
reminder call.

## Upselling

Offer the Club to every non-member after a repair over $250, and always after a second repair
within a year. Never pressure a customer during an emergency call.
