# SB-2025-03: AVT-199 Flow Sensor Scaling

## Affected units

Aquavane AVT-199 tankless water heaters built before January 2025 (serial numbers AVT199-24 and
earlier).

## The problem

The original flow sensor's turbine collects scale in hard water. The unit then under-reads the
flow, fires too low, and the customer gets lukewarm water, sometimes with an E-16 code.

## The fix

Replace the flow sensor with the updated FS-199B, which has a coated turbine and a finer inlet
screen. Descale the heat exchanger at the same visit if the unit hasn't been descaled in the past
year.

## Coverage

Aquavane supplies FS-199B free for affected serial numbers. Labour is covered for units still
inside the 1-year labour warranty only.
