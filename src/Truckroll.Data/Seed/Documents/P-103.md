# P-103 Gas Smell and Carbon Monoxide Calls

Safety comes before booking. The CSR reads these steps to the caller before anything else.

## Gas smell

1. Tell the caller to leave the house now, leave the door open, and not use light switches,
   phones or anything electrical inside.
2. From outside, they call Enbridge Gas's emergency line or 911.
3. Book an Emergency job (P-101) for after the utility has made the house safe.

## Carbon monoxide alarm

1. Everyone out of the house, including pets. Call 911 from outside.
2. Do not go back in until the fire department says it's safe.
3. Book an Emergency job: a G2 or G1 technician inspects every fuel-burning appliance and the
   venting, and leaves a written report.

## Never

- Never tell a caller a gas smell is "probably nothing".
- Never have a caller relight anything after a gas smell.
- Never book a gas-smell call as anything lower than Emergency.
