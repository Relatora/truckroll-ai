# Kestrel Home Comfort Workmanship Guarantee

Our own promise, on top of the manufacturer's warranty.

## Installations

We guarantee our installation workmanship for **5 years**. If something fails because of how we
installed it (a leaking joint, a wiring fault, a venting problem), we fix it at no charge, parts
and labour.

## Repairs

Repairs are guaranteed for **1 year**, parts and labour. If the same part fails again within a
year, the second repair is free.

Repeat failures of the same part are also a signal: if a customer's equipment has the same
failure a third time, the branch manager reviews it and we consider whether the unit has a known
defect (check service bulletins) or needs replacing.

## Comfort Club members

Members get labour covered on any manufacturer-warranty repair, so a warranty part costs them
nothing at all. See policy P-102.
