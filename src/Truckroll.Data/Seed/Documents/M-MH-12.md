# Mistral MH-12 Bypass Humidifier

A flow-through bypass humidifier mounted on the furnace supply or return plenum. Water runs over
an evaporator panel; furnace airflow picks up the moisture. No licence needed; it's a plumbing
and low-voltage connection.

## Setting the humidistat

Indoor humidity should drop as it gets colder outside, or windows sweat. A good rule:

| Outdoor temperature | Indoor humidity setting |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- |
| -30 °C              | 15%                     |
| -20 °C              | 20%                     |
| -10 °C              | 30%                     |
| 0 °C and warmer     | 35 to 40%               |

## Troubleshooting

### Not adding moisture

1. Is the bypass damper open (winter position)?
2. Is the water panel hard and crusted? Replace it (WP-12). Panels should be replaced every
   heating season.
3. Is the solenoid valve opening? Check for 24 V at the valve when the humidistat calls, and
   clean the inlet strainer.

### Water dripping from the drain

The drain line must slope to a floor drain or a condensate pump and must not be kinked.

## Maintenance

Replace the water panel each fall and clean the distribution tray and drain.
