# P-106 Customer Follow-Ups After a Call

## When to follow up

Send a short follow-up email after every call that books a job, and after any call where the
customer asked a question we couldn't answer on the phone.

## What it says

- Thank them by name.
- Repeat what we understood the problem to be, in their words.
- Confirm the booked window, or say when we'll call back.
- For a repeat problem, acknowledge it plainly ("we know this is the third time").
- Remind members of what their plan covers for this visit (P-102), if anything.
- Sign off with the branch name and phone number.

Keep it under 120 words. No marketing in a follow-up.

## Approval

Follow-ups drafted by Truckroll's assistant are drafts. A CSR reads every one and edits it
before it's sent. Nothing goes to a customer automatically.
