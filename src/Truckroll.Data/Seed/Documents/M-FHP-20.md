# Frostline FHP-20 Cold-Climate Heat Pump

A variable-speed inverter heat pump rated to heat down to -26 °C. Usually installed with a gas
furnace as backup (dual fuel). Sizes FHP-20-24, FHP-20-36 and FHP-20-48. Refrigerant work
requires a 313A licence.

## How dual fuel switches over

Below the balance point (set in the thermostat, factory default -12 °C) the heat pump stops and
the furnace takes over. Customers sometimes call this a fault; it isn't.

## Error codes (outdoor unit display)

| Code | Meaning                         | First check                               |
| ---- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| d1   | Defrost sensor open or shorted  | Defrost thermistor DS-20                   |
| d4   | Defrost took longer than 15 min | Airflow over the coil, snow around unit    |
| P3   | High pressure lockout (cooling) | Outdoor coil dirty, fan motor              |
| L2   | Low pressure lockout            | Refrigerant charge                         |

## Troubleshooting

### Outdoor unit covered in ice

Some frost in winter is normal; the unit defrosts itself every 30 to 90 minutes. A solid block of
ice means it isn't defrosting. Check the defrost thermistor (about 10 kΩ at 25 °C). Also make
sure the unit sits at least 30 cm above the snow line and that roof runoff doesn't drip onto it.

### Lukewarm air in heating mode

Check the reversing valve is shifting fully. A sticking valve can sometimes be freed by cycling
between heating and cooling; if it sticks again, replace it.

## Maintenance

Twice a year: clean the outdoor coil, check the stand and clearances, test defrost, record
pressures and temperatures in both modes, and confirm the dual fuel balance point with the
homeowner.
