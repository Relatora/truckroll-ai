# Hearthmark HMB-110 Combination Boiler

A wall-hung condensing combination boiler: space heating for radiators or in-floor heat, plus
on-demand domestic hot water. 110,000 BTU/h input. Gas work requires a G2 licence.

## Normal operating values

| Reading                    | Normal                     |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| System pressure, cold      | 1.0 to 1.5 bar (12 to 20 psi) |
| Expansion tank pre-charge  | 12 psi                     |
| Supply temperature, radiators | 60 to 80 °C             |

## Troubleshooting

### Pressure keeps dropping

If the pressure gauge falls overnight and the system needs refilling often, look for:

1. A waterlogged expansion tank. Tap it: the top half should sound hollow. Isolate it, drain the
   system side and recharge the air side to 12 psi.
2. The pressure relief valve weeping. Check its discharge pipe for water.
3. A leak in the system: radiator valves, fittings, or in-floor manifolds.

### Radiators cold at the top or upstairs

Air in the system. Bleed each radiator starting from the lowest floor, then top the pressure
back up to 1.2 bar.

### No hot water but heating works

Check the domestic hot water flow sensor and the plate heat exchanger, which scales in hard water.

## Maintenance

Yearly: check combustion, clean the condensate trap, check the expansion tank charge, test the
relief valve, and record system pressure.
