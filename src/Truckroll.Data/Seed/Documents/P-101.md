# P-101 Emergency Service and After-Hours Calls

## What counts as an emergency

- No heat when the outdoor temperature is below 0 °C.
- No heat in a home with an infant, a senior, or someone medically vulnerable, at any temperature.
- Any gas smell, or a carbon monoxide alarm sounding. **These follow P-103 first.**
- Active water leak or a burst pipe.
- No cooling when Environment Canada has issued a heat warning, for homes with vulnerable people.

Everything else is Urgent (within 48 hours) or Normal.

## Response targets

| Priority  | Target                                        |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Emergency | Technician on site within 4 hours, same day   |
| Urgent    | Within 2 business days                        |
| Normal    | Next available slot                           |

Emergencies go to the front of the board. A dispatcher may move a Normal job to make room, and
must call that customer to rebook.

## After-hours

After 6 pm on weekdays, and on weekends and statutory holidays, an after-hours fee of $149
applies on top of the repair. **Comfort Club Plus members pay no after-hours fee**; Comfort Club
members pay half.

## Licences

A gas emergency must go to a technician with a G2 or G1 licence. A G3 alone can't attend.
