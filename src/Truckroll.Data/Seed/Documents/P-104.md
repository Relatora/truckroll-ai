# P-104 Warranty Registration and Claims

## Registration

The installing technician registers every new unit with the manufacturer before leaving the job,
and notes "warranty registered" on the job. Unregistered units lose half their parts coverage
(see W-CAL, W-FRO).

## When a part fails under warranty

1. The technician checks the serial number against the warranty end date in Truckroll.
2. Use a part from truck stock to fix it today. Don't make the customer wait for the
   manufacturer.
3. Note the failed part's number and the serial number on the job.
4. The branch's office files the claim within 5 business days.

## Repeat failures

When the same part fails on the same unit for the third time, the technician notes it and the
branch manager reviews it. Check for a service bulletin first (for example SB-2024-07 for
Calderon CAL-80 ignitors). If the unit is in a bulletin's range, file under the bulletin, which
usually extends coverage and pays labour.

## Customer charges

Warranty parts are free to the customer. Labour is charged at the normal rate, except for
Comfort Club members (P-102) and our own workmanship guarantee (W-KHC).
